## Denmark: SIRI: higher education residence permit
Source: https://nyidanmark.dk/en-GB/You-want-to-apply/Study/Higher-Education/
+ The work permit is also valid during the job seeking period that is normally granted to you if you expect to complete a full educational programme in Denmark.
+ If you are a Turkish citizen covered by the Association Agreement between the EU and Turkey and you have applied for or been granted a residence permit as a student in a higher educational programme, you can be granted a limited work permit even if you submitted your application on or after 2 May 2025.
+ If you have completed an entire state-approved higher educational programme in Denmark, you can be granted a residence permit to seek employment in Denmark. This residence permit is called a job seeking permit. The duration of your job seeking permit depends on the grounds for your residence as a student and may be 6 months, 1 year or 3 years.
+ If you are granted a residence permit for a state-approved educational programme leading to a Danish degree, and you applied for a residence permit before 1 October 2026, you will generally be granted a 6-month or 3-year job seeking permit together with your residence permit.
+ If you applied for a residence permit on or after 1 October 2026 and obtain a Danish academy profession degree, professional bachelor’s, bachelor’s, part-time master’s or master’s degree, or complete an master’s degree programme for working professionals in a state-approved educational programme, you will generally be granted a 6-month or 1-year job seeking permit together with your residence permit.
+ If you obtain a Danish PhD degree, you will generally be granted a 3-year job seeking permit together with your residence permit.
+ If you are enrolled in a non-state-approved Danish higher educational programme and you applied for a residence permit before 2 May 2025, you will be eligible for a 6-month job seeking permit after you have completed your education. If you applied for a residence permit to study in a non-state-approved educational programme on or after 2 May 2025, you will not be eligible for a job seeking permit after you have completed your education.
+ If you are a Turkish citizen covered by the Association Agreement between the EU and Turkey and you have applied for or been granted a residence permit as a student in a higher educational programme below PhD level, you may be entitled to a job-seeking permit for either 6 months or 3 years. This applies even if you applied for a residence permit on or after 2 May 2025 on the basis of a non-state-approved educational programme or on or after 1 October 2026 on the basis of a state-approved educational programme.
+ The job seeking permit gives you the same work rights as you had during your studies: 90 hours per month and full-time through June, July and August. The hours are calculated per calender month.
+ A residence and work permit as a student in a PhD programme in Denmark allows your family to live with you in Denmark. A residence permit can only be granted to your spouse, registered partner or cohabiting partner, as well as children under the age of 18 who live at home.
+ If you applied for a residence permit before 1 October 2026 as a student in a state-approved higher educational programme leading to a Danish academy profession degree, professional bachelor’s, bachelor’s, part-time master’s or master’s degree, and your spouse, cohabiting partner or registered partner applied for a residence permit before 1 October 2026, they can be granted a residence permit as your accompanying family member. If your spouse, cohabiting partner or registered partner submitted their application on or after 1 October 2026, they cannot be granted a residence permit as your accompanying family member.
+ Your children under the age of 18 can apply for a residence permit as your accompanying family members if you submitted your application for a residence permit as a student in a state-approved higher educational programme before 1 October 2026.
